Frequently Asked Questions

How long does shipping take?

Most orders ship within 2-3 business days. Domestic shipping takes 5-7 business days. Express shipping options are available.

Do you offer custom orders?

Yes! Many of our artisans accept custom commissions. Contact us with your idea and we'll connect you with the right artisan.

What is your return policy?

We offer a 30-day return policy for unused items in original condition. Custom orders are final sale.

Can I visit your studio?

Absolutely! Our studio is open to the public during business hours. We'd love to show you around.
